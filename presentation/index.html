The ethics line
06 / 10

“Dark patterns for your benefit” is a dangerous brief

Pressure,
not deception.

Used, transparently
  • Earned scarcity — 3/7/14/30 scheduled days or 1/2/4/8 weeks
  • Real loss aversion — a real flame, after 20:00
  • Goal gradient — the actual distance to the next slot
  • Commitment — a cue, a time, a one-line reason
  • Recovery — a shield that covers the previous missed period
Refused
  • Confirm-shaming and guilt copy
  • Fake urgency and manufactured countdowns
  • Invented social proof
  • Pre-ticked consent and hidden costs
  • A roach motel for your own data
If the user could read the mechanic and still choose it, it stays. Settings → “How Ember persuades” prints the whole list.
Dark-pattern taxonomy: Gray et al., CHI 2018 · Mathur et al., CSCW 2019.

Timed rituals
07 / 10

Honest about the platform

A reminder that
knows what it
cannot do.

◉

App open: a real notification

A minute tick checks timed, unlit rituals. Permission is requested from a user gesture, never on load. If it is denied, the same message becomes an in-app alert.

Service worker · once per day
▤

App closed: the calendar

A closed serverless PWA cannot schedule a local notification. One tap exports recurring .ics alarms for every timed ritual.

OS-level alarm · no server
iOS 16.4+ web push requires a Home Screen app and a push server. Ember has no server, so it does not promise push. That is why the calendar export exists.
